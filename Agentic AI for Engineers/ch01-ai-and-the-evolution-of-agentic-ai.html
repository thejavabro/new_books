AI and the Evolution of Agentic AI

A chatbot answers. An agent gets things done. This chapter maps the branches of AI that agents are built from, introduces the loop at the heart of every agent, and follows automation from frozen rules to systems you hand a goal.

1

Same question, two kinds of AI

Ask about a card balance that is due. A reactive chatbot replies and stops. An agent keeps going: it looks across accounts, proposes a plan, waits for you, then acts. Switch between them and press play.

Prompt in, answer out. No memory, no initiative, no action.

2

AI is a constellation of capabilities

AI is not one technique. It is a family of abilities that each copy a slice of human intelligence. Machine learning sits inside AI, deep learning inside that, and generative AI is a capability built on deep learning. Agentic AI is drawn as a layer across all of them, because it coordinates them toward a goal. Tap any region.

3

Three ways a machine learns

Supervised learning studies flashcards with the answers on the back. Unsupervised learning sorts a pile of unlabeled toys into groups on its own. Reinforcement learning is a toddler learning to walk: try, fall, adjust, and chase the reward over the long run. Pick a style and run it.

4

Feature engineering: the impossible journey

Raw data holds a timestamp and a location per card swipe. Neither looks suspicious alone. Combine them into a new feature, travel speed between swipes, and a stolen card used on two continents an hour apart lights up. Domain knowledge turns columns into signals.

Deep learning peels the onion

A deep network stacks layers. Early layers notice edges, middle layers assemble shapes, deep layers recognize the thing itself. No one hand-writes these features: the network learns them from raw pixels, sound or words. Step through the layers.

Generative AI creates. Agentic AI acts.

Transformers made generative AI possible, and generative models became the reasoning core of agents. But a model alone waits for a prompt. An agent is a whole system around the model. Flip each row to compare.

7

The heartbeat: perceive, reason, act, learn

Every agent runs a loop. Give one the goal "trim this month's cloud bill" and watch a single lap. The language model is the reasoning core; around it sit data sources, tools and a feedback channel that makes the next lap smarter.

8

Automation's parallel road

While AI learned to see and speak, automation was growing up too. Each era handled more variety, but each also broke in its own way. Move the slider, then throw the same surprise at every era: the vendor changed its invoice layout.

9

From MRKL to ReAct: thinking out loud with tools

Early agent frameworks such as MRKL let a model call tools to answer a question and then stop. ReAct added an explicit loop: write a thought, take an action, read the observation, repeat until done. A model without tools would guess at live data. A ReAct agent goes and gets it. (Company and numbers are invented.)

10

A digital coworker that knows when to call you

A support agent can handle a locked-account call end to end: transcribe, verify identity, check policy, read the caller's mood, fix it. Good design also tells it when to hand over. Set the risk signals and see the route it takes.
