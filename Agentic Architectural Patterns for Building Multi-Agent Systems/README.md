# Agentic Architectural Patterns for Building Multi-Agent Systems — visual companion

Interactive, illustrated learning pages for *Agentic Architectural Patterns for Building Multi-Agent Systems* (Dr. Ali Arsanjani & Juan Pablo Bustos, Packt 2026), one self-contained HTML page per chapter (16 chapters).

Open `index.html` in any browser. Every page works offline (fonts fall back to system fonts) and links to the others by relative path, so keep the files together in one folder, e.g. `C:\repo\new_books\Agentic Architectural Patterns for Building Multi-Agent Systems\`.

- 16 chapters grouped by the book's three parts, light and dark themes, phone friendly
- Step-through animated diagrams, pattern explorers, simulations (routers, voting, watchdogs, loan pipelines, framework comparisons), quizzes and a one-page recap per chapter
- Explanations are original summaries with redrawn diagrams, not text from the book. Numbers marked "illustrative" are not from the book.
- Chapter 1's body text was missing from the source file, so its page is rebuilt from the table of contents, the Chapter 16 recap and cross-references in later chapters.

Chapters: 1 GenAI in the Enterprise · 2 Agent-Ready LLMs · 3 LLM Adaptation Spectrum · 4 Agentic Architecture · 5 Multi-Agent Coordination · 6 Explainability and Compliance · 7 Robustness and Fault Tolerance · 8 Human-Agent Interaction · 9 Agent-Level Patterns · 10 System-Level Patterns · 11 Agents That Learn · 12 Practical Roadmap · 13 Single-Agent Loan Case · 14 Multi-Agent Loan Case · 15 Agent Frameworks · 16 Charting Your Journey

`_source/` holds the shared CSS/JS, chapter sources and `build.py` (`python3 build.py out`) for regenerating the pages.
