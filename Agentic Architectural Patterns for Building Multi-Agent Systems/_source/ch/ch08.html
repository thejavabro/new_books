Each pattern is an arrow: who initiates, and who acts. Click an arrow.

Adopt the patterns progressively. Each level unlocks a new kind of hand-off.

The loan agent runs autonomously above 95% confidence. An appraisal says 1,800 sq ft; tax records say 1,500. Move the threshold, then play the underwriter.

A good escalation package has the reason, the evidence, the options, and what the agent recommends, so the human decides in one minute instead of re-doing the work. The workflow pauses and resumes with the human's validated answer.

"Where is my order?" is a call: one intent, one tool, one answer. "Draft a competitor report on ProWidget X in Europe by tomorrow" is a delegation: the agent plans and works alone for a while. Compare.

Inside the system, a primary agent acts as project manager and hides a team of specialists. At the edge, a proxy agent is the only one holding the keys to an external system. Step through a comprehensive financial analysis and a cross-enterprise loyalty lookup.

"Book me a trip to the New York office next week for Q3 planning. Refundable flight, corporate hotel." Press play and watch each pattern take its turn.
