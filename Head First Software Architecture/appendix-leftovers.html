5

Depth vs breadth

Picture all knowledge as a pyramid: things you know, things you know you don't know, and the huge base of things you don't know you don't know. Move through a career and watch the shape change.

Knowing five ways to solve a problem, and their trade-offs, is worth more to an architect than knowing one way very deeply. Some of your depth will fade, and that's normal.
6

Practise with katas

In martial arts a kata is a form you repeat until it's perfect. An architectural kata is a practice design session: small teams of three to five, a moderator playing the customer, and a made-up project. Step through a session, then draw a practice brief.
