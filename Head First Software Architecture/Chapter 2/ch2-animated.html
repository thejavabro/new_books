What Are Architectural Characteristics?

The domain is the problem you're solving (why you're writing software). But architects must also analyze the capabilities the system needs beyond the domain — these are architectural characteristics.

Three defining properties:

1
Nondomain design considerations — they specify how to implement requirements, not what the app does.
2
Influence architectural structure — they require special structural support to succeed.
3
Critical or important to application success — without them, the system fails its users.

Three Dangerous Properties

Architectural characteristics are powerful but tricky. Here's why you must limit how many you choose:

🔗

Synergistic

Changing one affects others. More security → worse performance.

🌊

Overabundant

New ones appear constantly. The list never ends.

🏷️

Unstandardized

No universal list. Terms vary between companies.

Three Sources of Characteristics

Where do architectural characteristics come from? Not just requirements — look in three places.

📋 Problem Domain

"Thousands of users" → dig deeper: How many at once? How fast? This gives you scalability, concurrency, and elasticity.

🌍 Environmental Awareness

Fast-moving startup? Prioritize agility. Enterprise doing mergers? Prioritize interoperability.

🧠 Holistic Domain Knowledge

University sign-ups won't be even — Type A students rush early, procrastinators pile at the end. Design for elastic bursts.

Ask "But why?!" like an annoying toddler. The F-16 was designed because someone asked why the jet needed Mach 2.5 — the real need was to flee a fight, not hit a speed number.

Chapter Summary

1. Architectural characteristics define a system's capabilities — what it can do beyond its domain functionality.
2. They come from three sources: the problem domain, environmental awareness, and holistic domain knowledge.
3. Characteristics are synergistic, overabundant, and unstandardized.
4. Some are explicit, others implicit. Some are composites of others.
5. Limit to about seven, prioritize the top three, and translate business language into architectural characteristics.
