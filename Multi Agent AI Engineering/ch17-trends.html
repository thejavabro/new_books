A model upgrade, and two gates that looked alike

The chapter opens with a team whose system had two blocking steps. One forced the model to decompose drafts into small checked pieces. The other was an approval gate before publication. After a model upgrade, the decomposition step no longer helped, so the team removed it. The approval gate stayed.

Both stopped the model and imposed structure, but for different reasons. The first was a capability workaround. The second carried a product obligation: the organisation decides what is published under its name, keeps the evidence and can recover from mistakes. A better model retired the first and made the second matter more.

That is the chapter's lens on every trend: as learned capability grows, a boundary moves, but some responsibility always stays explicit. Pick a theme.

Why small errors dominate long tasks

If each of n steps succeeds independently with probability p and any failure ruins the task, success is pⁿ. So the longest task you can finish with success rate q is n = log(q) / log(p). Small gains in per-step reliability stretch the horizon a lot.

69 steps is the longest task at that target

The architecture does not have to accept a task's apparent length. Its effective horizon is the longest chain a component must carry without a reliable checkpoint or recovery point. A hundred-step workflow with checked milestones can have a shorter effective horizon than ten steps held in one generated trace. Durable execution and honest completion (state, progress and "done" visible outside the model) matter more as runs stretch to weeks.

If four agents use four times the compute, compare with the best single agent that budget can buy: a stronger model, more search, more verification, or several samples. In the scaling study cited in Chapter 11, adding agents began to hurt once one agent already solved more than about 45% of a task family.

Authorisation is not intent

"The agent" is several identities: the principal it acts for, the tenant whose policy applies, the agent service, the model version, the host, the instance, the run, and any sub-agents or tools it delegates to. Every identity that changes an authorisation decision needs a traceable binding.

Infrastructure as planes of responsibility

The emerging agent stack is easier to reason about as six planes. They are separations of responsibility, not necessarily six products. A durable run crosses all of them. Tap a plane.

Tap a plane.

Agents are also becoming first-class consumers of software. A service used by agents must state its business invariants, capacity limits and support at machine pace. Pricing moves with the unit of value: each meter (seats, actions, tokens, outcomes) aligns with one thing and distorts another. A per-seat price hides autonomous volume; a per-action price rewards splitting or bundling actions. When agents trade with agents, mechanism design becomes part of engineering.

6

Why does each component exist?

Classify each material component by the reason it exists, and record it in an assumption register with a retest trigger and an owner. Then, when the models change, you know what to re-test and what to keep. Sort these.

What survives every model upgrade is a set of obligations: business state has a source of truth; authority comes from an accountable principal and narrows through delegation; consequential effects leave evidence; failures have a recovery path; evaluation measures what the product claims; spending has a limit and an owner; and people keep the decisions and recourse assigned to them.

Recap

  • Each trend moves a boundary between learned capability and explicit responsibility. The responsibility does not vanish.
  • Long horizons: success is pⁿ, so shorten the effective horizon with checkpoints and honest completion.
  • Learned teamwork can fake consensus. Beat the strongest single agent at the same budget.
  • Bind approvals to exact terms; ask only when a choice is consequential.
  • Think in six planes: execution, context, model, tool, evidence, control.
  • Keep an assumption register: workarounds retire, obligations stay, hedges are re-checked.
