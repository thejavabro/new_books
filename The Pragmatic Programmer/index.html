How to use these pagesThe book is a collection of topics you can read in any order, and so are these pages. Each one covers a chapter (or half of a long one), opens with the big idea, then gives every topic a drawing or a toy to play with. Tip numbers match the book. Your theme choice is the only thing saved, and only in your browser.
