# Apache Kafka in Action — Visual Companion

An interactive, offline-friendly companion to *Apache Kafka in Action: From basics to production* by Anatoly Zelenin and Alexander Kropp (Manning, 2025). There is one self-contained HTML page per chapter. Each page has clickable diagrams, small simulations (partitioning, replication, consumer groups, compaction, windows, quotas, failover and more), a recap and a short quiz. Every page has light and dark themes and works at phone width.

## How to open

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No server or internet connection is needed. Google Fonts load when you are online; otherwise the pages fall back to system fonts.

## Note on content

All explanations, diagrams and simulations are original summaries written for this companion. They are not reproductions of the book's text or figures. Short configuration snippets and commands are shown only where they are needed for understanding. Simulations are simplified teaching models, and prices on the cost sliders are illustrative assumptions.

## Missing content

None. All 18 chapters and both appendices are covered. The blank pages in the source PDF are layout pages at chapter ends; the index and back matter were not converted.
