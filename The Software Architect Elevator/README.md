# The Software Architect Elevator, visual companion

An interactive, illustrated study companion to *The Software Architect Elevator* by Gregor Hohpe (O'Reilly, 2020). It has 14 pages that cover all 41 chapters plus the epilogue. Each page includes SVG diagrams, small interactive models, a quiz and a one-page recap.

## How to open

Unzip it and open `index.html` in any modern browser. The pages are self-contained and work offline. Only the Google Fonts stylesheet is loaded from the web, and the pages fall back to system fonts without it. Light and dark themes follow your system setting.

## Pages

- `index.html`: overview and the chapter map
- `ch01-03-the-architect-elevator.html` — Chapters 1–3: The Architect Elevator (Riding between penthouse and engine room; movie personas; the first derivative)
- `ch04-07-enterprise-decisions.html` — Chapters 4–7: Architects in the Enterprise (Enterprise architecture, the three legs, making decisions, asking questions)
- `ch08-10-architecture-options.html` — Chapters 8–10: Architecture Is Selling Options (Spotting real architecture, decisions as options, systems that resist change)
- `ch11-12-code-and-zombies.html` — Chapters 11–12: Code and Zombies (Configuration is code too; retiring systems before they eat your budget)
- `ch13-15-automation-and-standards.html` — Chapters 13–15: Automate, Version, Standardize (Self-service automation, software-defined everything, standards that enable)
- `ch16-17-flat-world-coffee-shop.html` — Chapters 16–17: The Flat World and the Coffee Shop (Vendor maps and abstraction; real-world lessons in asynchronous design)
- `ch18-20-explaining-and-writing.html` — Chapters 18–20: Explaining and Writing (Ramps not cliffs, the pirate ship, writing for busy readers)
- `ch21-23-diagrams-and-lines.html` — Chapters 21–23: Diagrams That Mean Something (Emphasis over completeness, diagram-driven design, why lines matter)
- `ch24-25-sketching-and-collaboration.html` — Chapters 24–25: Sketching and Collaboration (Drawing out the architecture people have in their heads; git as a model)
- `ch26-28-reverse-engineering-orgs.html` — Chapters 26–28: Reverse-Engineering Organizations (How organizations really work, the illusion of control, old structures)
- `ch29-32-scaling-and-governance.html` — Chapters 29–32: Scaling, Discipline, Governance (Black markets, scaling like a distributed system, speed needs discipline)
- `ch33-35-pain-change-speed.html` — Chapters 33–35: No Pain, No Change (Why change needs pain, leading by example, economies of speed)
- `ch36-38-loops-and-culture.html` — Chapters 36–38: Loops, Inside-Out, Culture (Build-measure-learn, being digital on the inside, buying culture)
- `ch39-41-queues-dimensions-truth.html` — Chapters 39–41: Queues, Dimensions, Truth (Waiting in line, thinking in four dimensions, the red pill)

## Note

All explanations, diagrams and quizzes are original summaries and illustrations written for study. They are not text from the book, and some examples and numbers are illustrative. Read the book for the full argument.
