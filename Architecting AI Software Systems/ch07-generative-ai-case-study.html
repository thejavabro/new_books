Architecting a Generative AI System: A Case Study

2

The vision and the indexing pipeline

The executive team's plan rests on five pillars: a unified semantic index across all repositories, grounded answers via retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), contextual understanding of domain language, an adaptive learning loop, and structured connectors to CRM, ERP and ITSM systems (read-only, row-level security, rate limits, exposed to the agent as typed tool calls or SQL views) so answers cite documents and live system facts. Step through how a document becomes searchable.

3

From business goals to data science targets

Business goals were translated into five measurable data science objectives, with auditability, explainability and confidence reporting aligned to AI risk-management guidance (NIST AI RMF). These targets later drive model choice, evaluation and architecture.

4

The LLM tier: right model for each query

The LLM interprets queries, plans retrieval, integrates context and writes the answer. TechSolve uses three tiers behind an abstraction layer that standardises inputs and outputs, so models can be switched by complexity, cost or compliance and the company avoids vendor lock-in. Route a query.

The book notes a 70B-parameter local fallback needs serious GPU memory, often 4- or 8-bit quantisation and pruning; many firms find 7B or 13B models an adequate, far cheaper fallback.

5

The vector database and hybrid retrieval

Embeddings turn each chunk into a long vector (the book's example model produces 1,536 dimensions) so that chunks with similar meaning sit close together even if the wording differs. TechSolve indexes 16 million chunks across six indexes in Pinecone (Qdrant is the open-source alternative mentioned), combines vector similarity with BM25 keyword matching, and filters on 14 metadata fields. The book credits RAG with an 87% hallucination reduction in this case. Try a toy version: blend semantic and keyword scores and filter by product version.

Metadata filter

Some teams also explore context-aware generation (CAG), which optimises how context is used inside the model to cut redundant retrieval calls and cost. For anything newer than the model's training data, a web search tool (Google Programmable Search behind a wrapper with rate limits, caching, credibility ranking, extraction and summarisation) fills gaps.

6

The agent workflow in six stages

A LangChain agent turns a single model into an orchestrator: it keeps conversation memory, decomposes problems, calls tools (retrieval, web search, calculators, APIs) and records its reasoning for audit. More agent autonomy means less deterministic output, so guardrails (constraints and validation checks) keep answers consistent with business rules. Follow one query.

7

End-to-end architecture

Infrastructure is hybrid: Azure with managed Kubernetes for core processing, on-premises processing for regulated healthcare data, containers for dev and test, and cross-region disaster recovery with a 15-minute recovery point objective. Cost is managed with autoscaling, reserved instances and utilisation monitoring. The software follows a tiered model. Click a tier.

Use case: query resolutionThe support engineer signs in with SSO, describes the customer's problem, the agent retrieves knowledge (and web results if needed), the LLM answers with citations, the engineer reviews and edits, and the answer is attached to the ticket with everything logged for training. Variations: ask for clarification or flag a knowledge gap when information is thin; route sensitive data on-premises; enforce authorisation on restricted content.
8

Measuring the business impact

Credible results need a baseline: TechSolve captured six months of pre-deployment data from its ticketing system (Jira), post-interaction surveys (NPS, satisfaction), finance cost models and the system's own analytics, all in one dashboard. Toggle before and after (the book's case figures).

9

Three principles that travel

The overarching lesson: ground AI answers in verified organisational knowledge, keep provenance clear, and close the loop with feedback, while balancing innovation with governance and scale with reliability.
