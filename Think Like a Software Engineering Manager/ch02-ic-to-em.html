Individual Contributor to Engineering Manager

Moving from writing code to leading the people who write it is a career change, not a reward. Go in for the right reasons, prepare in phases, and expect your definition of a good day to change.

Two different jobs, not two rungs

An individual contributor (IC) adds value directly, without managing anyone. An engineering manager manages engineers; a manager of managers is a senior manager. The move changes where your hours go. Switch between roles to see the rough shift the chapter describes: coding fades and leadership takes over as you climb the management track.

Separate ladders, sideways moves

Tech companies usually run parallel IC and EM ladders. You can be promoted on either. Moving from IC to EM at a similar level is a lateral change, and moving back is possible too, most easily at lower levels and if you keep your coding skills fresh. Hover or tap a rung.

4

A three-phase transition

Many companies let you try the role before committing. The author splits the move into three phases. Pick a phase and tick off the moves as you plan yours; your ticks are remembered on this device.

Taking over a team: three casesA different team: respect existing dynamics, change things gradually, never criticise the previous leader, do your homework. A new team from scratch: set a clear scope and mission, lean on your network and recruiters, and resist mass hiring; the author suggests doing this later in your management journey. Your own team: your former peers now report to you, so build trust patiently, watch for old biases and favouritism, and open a candid feedback channel.

6

Helping someone else make the move

Once you are established, you will coach others through the same transition: understand their motives, run a gap analysis, set a time-boxed 30-60-90 day plan and help them build a network. The author's network trick is simple: list everyone you will manage, meet each one, ask what hurts and what works, then ask for three more people you should talk to. Add anyone new to the list and repeat. Press play to watch it snowball.
