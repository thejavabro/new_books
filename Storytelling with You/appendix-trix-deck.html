The TRIX case study, start to finish

Every chapter of the book ends with the same project: advising a snack maker on a costly trail-mix recipe. Here is that thread in one place, ending with a walk-through of the final deck.

1

One project, twelve chapters

The case is based on real events with details changed. A market-research consultant has run a pilot for Nosh, a food maker, on its TRIX trail mix. Macadamia nuts, a signature ingredient, are getting expensive. The team tested packaging and two alternative recipes. If the presentation goes well, Nosh may hand the firm all its research work. Tap a chapter to see what happened at that stage.

Walking through the final deck

The book's appendix reprints 42 slides from the final presentation (minus some animation steps and the deck's own appendix). They were made to support a spoken talk, not to stand alone. Below are simplified redrawings of a selection, in order, with what each slide is doing. Values on the liking scale are approximate.

3

Lessons visible in the deck

Title = repeatable phrase"The magic is in the macadamia nuts" opens and closes the deck.
Goal stated up frontShare findings, then frame a discussion of price vs. preference.
Takeaway titles throughoutRead the titles alone and you get the story.
Images that earn their placeCustom photos of each mix, the macadamias circled.
Familiar to novelBars become dots, so the dot plot never feels foreign.
Sparing colorNavy for the original, bright blue for alternatives, orange only for the problem.
Ends on actionThree options to weigh, then the title slide again during discussion.
Rigor in reserveFull methods in an appendix, ready for the CFO or the scientists.

Source note: the appendix pages in the PDF are images without a text layer. The redrawings here are based on looking at those images; they are simplified and not reproductions.
