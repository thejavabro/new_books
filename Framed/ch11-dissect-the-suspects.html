Case file: a maid and an anonymous tipster

What happens in the story
  • Larry and Phil look for people who were around Laura's building. They talk to the maid who cleans it, Angela, who seems uncomfortable.
  • An anonymous email arrives from someone signing as Peeping Tom, with a tip about Paul Gerber's past. Whoever it is seems to know it's a murder case before anyone else does.
  • The list of possible suspects starts to take shape: Paul, who was pursuing Laura; Donald, who argued with her; and, Phil insists, anyone they might be too fond of to suspect.
2

Forward vs. backward imagining

Ask "could Paul have done it?" and the mind often answers yes or no. Ask "Paul did it. How did it happen?" and it starts producing stories. Toggle the framing and expand the tree.

3

The halo effect

Ten observers rate a stranger on four traits. Change only the person's looks and watch the other ratings move, though nothing else about them changed. (Paul Gerber, "down from Mount Olympus" with a volcanic temper, is a halo trap in both directions.)

Only appearance changed. The halo leaks into honesty, intelligence and kindness, which is why investigators, interviewers and juries need evidence that stands apart from impressions.

4

Regression to the mean

Each student's score = true skill + luck on the day. Pick the top group on test 1, then give everyone test 2. Their average falls, with no change in skill.
