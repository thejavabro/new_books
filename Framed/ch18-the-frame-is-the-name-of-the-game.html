The Frame is the Name of the Game

With hours left before the press conference, Larry, Phil and Shawn recalibrate the suspects and then design a scenario to make the killer reveal himself. The mantra flips: this time, they set the frame.

Case file: the restaurant

What happens in the story
  • Phil drives at 80 mph to police headquarters while Larry reworks the grid on paper.
  • Shawn has a plan: email each suspect separately, as if from someone who knows about the electric blanket. They improve it until it might work.
  • From Angela's own computer, the email goes to all three suspects: the cops are pressuring her, she needs $30,000 to flee to her sick daughter in Mexico tonight. Meet at Fernando's on Halsted at 7:30 PM.
  • Watching on monitors from a van, they see a man in a low hat sit down across from Angela. Larry gasps: he knows the back of that head.
2

Rescoring with the new anchor

Opportunity scores after shifting the window to 5–9 PM: Donald 3 → 9 (no alibi), Paul 9 → 6 (seen around 6), Professor 1 → 8 (dining room at 5:30, airport check-in 8:10, gaps between). Animate the shift.

Phil and Larry's honest caveat: the alibi information is imprecise, motives aren't understood, and the cocktail club and Donald's quarrel aren't even in the grid yet. "At this point we're guessing." A ranking under ambiguity is a guide to investigation, not a verdict.
