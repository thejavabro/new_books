1

Case file: the letter

What happens in the story
  • Ten days after the cell visit, a letter arrives. Martin admits he may have lost frame control, says he killed two people and has nothing to show for it, and lists what his students did right. "You framed me with the concepts I had taught you."
  • Phil notes it isn't an apology. Larry and Phil run an honest audit of their own investigation.
  • Shawn calls: Martin swallowed about sixty antidepressant capsules in his cell. He is in a coma, Donald at his side.

Recap on one page

What workedContrarian questions, reframing with new evidence, finding the weakest link, dropping the false anchor.
Mistake 1Values over facts: they couldn't evaluate the professor objectively.
Mistake 2Narrow frame: Diane's death and Donald's near miss were left outside the puzzle.
Mistake 3Conventional motives only (greed, anger); his mindset was in a class of its own.
Hindsight cautionClues look obvious afterwards. Phil warns Larry not to over-read one remembered sentence.
Thread's endMartin attempts suicide in his cell; his fate is left open until the epilogue.
