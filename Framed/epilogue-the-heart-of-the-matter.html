The Heart of the Matter

Back in San Francisco at 2 AM, Larry finishes the story for his brother Chris. The last lesson isn't another procedure. It's how to use the mind fully, and then let the heart and intuition in without letting biases in with them.

Case file: four years later

What happens in the story
  • Larry admits two things: he kept a diary so his telling wouldn't be bent by hindsight, and Phil deserves most of the credit for being the contrarian who started it all.
  • Phil died two years later, shot by accident while trying to talk a hostage-taker into releasing a child.
  • Larry runs his own software company and is about to go on his first date since Laura's death. Chris must decide about school and about his girlfriend, Linda, and wants Larry to decide for him.
