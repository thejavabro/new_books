Can You Spot Your Blots?

You can't become a good decision maker without facing your own mistakes. The test is not whether outcomes were good, but whether you used the information you had in the best way. That calls for an honest audit of your process.

Case file: a third argument

What happens in the story
  • Larry calls friends about Donald. Daniel at GO reveals that three days before her death, Laura had a heated argument with Donald in his office, and again it was about Larry.
  • Three incidents now share one thread. A pattern, or a random streak? Larry knows he can't judge it objectively, because he is in it.
  • In class the professor, arriving late and rushed, introduces the acrostic for the seven mantras and the decision audit, and leads the class to the five Cicor lessons: WISER.
  • Notes pass under the desks: Paul was in the library until 5 and in the Commons at 6. Donald left his office at 4:30 and nobody knows where he went. Shawn calls: a press conference is set for tomorrow. Time's up.
