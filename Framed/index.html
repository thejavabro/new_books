Framed!, Drawn Out

A murder mystery that teaches you how to make smart choices. One interactive page per chapter: case files, diagrams you can step through, small simulations and quick checks.

Spoiler note. The book is a whodunit. Chapters 18 to 20 and the epilogue reveal the solution, and are marked. Earlier pages stay spoiler-safe.
