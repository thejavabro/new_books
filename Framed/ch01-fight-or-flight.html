Case file: the night outside the theater

What happens in the story

Years after the events, Larry Rowe sits up late on a Friday with his younger brother Chris, who is facing big choices of his own. Instead of giving advice, Larry tells a story: the season he spent in Professor Martin Armstrong's decision-making seminar, and the death of the woman he loved.

  • It begins months earlier, outside a theater in Chicago. Laura Armstrong and her sister Diane are attacked by a mugger.
  • In a split second, Laura's instinct makes the choice for her: she runs. Diane is killed.
  • Laura carries that moment with her. She will never know whether fighting back would have saved her sister.

The book uses this opening to make a point it returns to at the very end: some choices are made by our biology before reason gets a vote. Everything after this chapter is about the other kind of decision, the kind where we have time to think.
