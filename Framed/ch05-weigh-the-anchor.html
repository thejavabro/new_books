Weigh the Anchor without Rancor

Our minds simplify by latching onto reference points, even ones we know are arbitrary. The trick is not to throw anchors away but to weigh them: ignore the arbitrary ones, and use good ones on purpose.

Case file: guilt, unease and an odd classmate

What happens in the story
  • Phil wants the accident recorded in his file and gives $20,000 from his pension to Mary Ellen. Larry calls it post-decision regret and warns him not to become Lord Jim, Conrad's hero who sacrificed his life to redeem one act of cowardice.
  • Laura confides that Paul Gerber keeps asking her out and bragged about a secretive "chemistry club" involving lethal chemicals and euthanasia. She refuses Larry's offer to talk to Paul.

  • The frame team discusses groupthink and anchoring at PACE. On Friday they all go to a blues concert. On Monday morning Phil pounds on Larry's door: something awful has happened.

Spin an arbitrary anchor

Anchors as a tool

Groupthink check

Clara wonders if PACE fell into groupthink. Laura says it isn't the classic pattern (think Bay of Pigs). Tick the symptoms you see in a team, and watch the meter.
