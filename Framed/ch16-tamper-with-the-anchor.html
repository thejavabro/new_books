Tamper with the Anchor

One fact anchored the whole investigation: the time of death. When it turns out someone tampered with it, every alibi and every score has to be rechecked. The lesson: know which assumptions your decision hangs on.

1

Case file: Angela talks

What happens in the story
  • Larry learns Paul belongs to the Cocktail Hour, an extreme euthanasia group with underground access to lethal chemicals. Suggestive, but not conclusive.
  • Shawn will soon take over officially. Phil proposes a bluff: tell Angela another suspect is being offered a plea deal. A one-player Prisoner's Dilemma.
  • Angela breaks. On Sunday a caller with a robotic, disguised voice offered her $50,000 and a green card. Just before 7 PM she went into Laura's room, where Laura was already dead, warmed the body with an electric blanket and rolled her onto the bed. At 11 PM she propped Laura in the chair, tried to put the needle in her hand, wiped the room, and put the blanket next to the shoes.
  • "Weigh the anchor without rancor!" The time of death was faked. The real window is 5 to 9 PM Sunday.
  • In the library Stewart rehearses a talk on scenario strategies for PACE, until Paul storms in and accuses Larry of snooping behind his back.

Fragility of decisionsDecisions rest on key facts. If those change, the decision may too.
Sensitivity analysisVary the key inputs and see whether the choice survives.
