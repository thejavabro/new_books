1

Enterprise search finds documents; people want answers

In nearly every process (a new hire checking benefits, a buyer onboarding a supplier, a seller hunting for case studies) people search across many systems. Keyword search engines such as Elasticsearch struggle with unstructured content, need constant index and taxonomy upkeep, return documents instead of answers, and miss meaning: "supplier onboarding process" does not match a page titled "vendor registration procedures". Compare the two approaches on a tiny corpus.

Asking your data in plain English

The world's data was projected to grow from 33 to 175 zettabytes between 2018 and 2025, yet getting insight still means specialists and waiting days. The chapter lists the pain:

GenAI adds natural-language querying, automatic insights, real-time answers, synthetic data and reports tailored to each audience. In Looker Studio you can type a question and get a chart. Specialists such as Defog and Fleet focus on text-to-SQL: turning a question into a query that joins the right tables.

On-brand content for every segment

  
Marketing teams use platforms such as Jasper (broad, fast copywriting from templates) and Typeface (enterprise brand consistency, multimodal content, publishing to Google, Salesforce, Meta). The business-logic layer grounds generation in brand voice with RAG, learns from past campaign performance, and personalizes at scale.
