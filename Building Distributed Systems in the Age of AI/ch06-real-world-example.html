Three layers, one platform

Spotting the fault in the telemetry

Every five seconds the scooter reports GPS, battery voltage, current, speed and acceleration. Midway, the motor cuts out for a few seconds, twice: voltage dips, current spikes to compensate, both recover. A model trained on past rides classifies the window as a known battery-connector fault. Play the ride.

4

An event bus is not just a queue

The same cloud technology may carry both, but the meaning differs. The event bus carries domain events (booking.created, ride.started); losing one breaks data integrity. Telemetry queues carry measurements; losing a batch is a pity, but the next readings fix the picture. So they get different retention and recovery guarantees. The bus is a delivery mechanism; the streaming platform is where processing happens. Simulate losing a batch.

5

Agents that propose, and agents that act

Two kinds of agent appear. A fleet agent blends stream sinks, weather and event calendars into recommendations for humans: cold evenings cut battery efficiency, a concert ends at 10 PM, so swap batteries on low scooters downtown before 6 PM. A customer experience agent acts on its own within policy. Try both.

Fleet agent: what needs a battery swap tonight?

Toy numbers. In the book's scenario the agent recommends swaps on dozens of downtown vehicles; humans make the final call on big operational moves.

Customer agent: does the apology need a human?

The policy in the book's story: small credits for clear service issues need no oversight. The thresholds here are invented to make the idea tangible.

The integration architecture

No single style covers everything. Each pattern works where it gives most value, and boundaries keep them apart. Test yourself: who should own each job?

Which layer owns it?

Sync where it must beBooking, fleet and payment talk synchronously because the reservation needs an immediate answer.
Async between islandsEvents decouple producers from consumers; new consumers join without touching producers.
Streams for telemetryMany consumers read the same flow for location, battery, anomalies and training data.
Agents across boundariesThey synthesize context spanning domains and call services; they never own vehicle state.
3D at the microscaleEach service is itself several processes arranged as in Chapter 2.
