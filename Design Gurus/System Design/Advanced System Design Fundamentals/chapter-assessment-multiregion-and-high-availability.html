Chapter Assessment: Multi-Region and Disaster Recovery

This is the chapter review for Multi-Region and Disaster Recovery. The bank holds twenty questions and each attempt draws ten, so repeating it gives you different coverage. This is the topic that invites over-building more than any other in the course, so the bank is weighted toward what keeps a design proportionate. The recurring questions are which of the two goals you are serving, which of the four arrangements the requirement calls for, and what to try before adding a second region at all. One question concerns what European data protection law actually requires about data location, and the widely held answer is wrong. Treat a wrong answer as a pointer to the lesson to reread.
