Pretrained AI APIs and Solutions

If you have no training data and no ML team, you can still add sight, language and speech to an app with a single API call. When you need more, AutoML and industry solutions sit on top.

Sight, language, conversation, structure

Google's pretrained APIs are models trained on huge generic datasets and maintained by Google; you call them with an API key and get JSON back. Each has an AutoML sibling in Vertex AI for when you need your own labels or domain vocabulary, and on top sit industry solutions that combine several APIs for one business problem. Pick a family to see the members.

2

Vision AI: what's in this image?

Vision API classifies images into thousands of predefined labels, detects and locates objects, faces and landmarks, reads printed and handwritten text (OCR), finds logos, flags explicit content with SafeSearch, and suggests crops and dominant colors. Product Search finds visually similar items in your catalog. When the labels you need aren't generic ("scratch on part 7"), train AutoML Vision on your own images. Video AI: search inside video

  
The Video Intelligence API annotates stored or streaming video: labels at video, shot and frame level, shot-change detection, object tracking, text (OCR) in frames, logo recognition, explicit-content detection, face and person detection and speech transcription. Every annotation comes with time offsets, so video becomes searchable. Use cases: content moderation, recommendations, searchable media archives, contextual ad placement. AutoML Video trains on your own categories.

Translation AI: say it in 100+ languages

  
Translation API detects the source language and translates text in real time; Advanced adds batch jobs, whole-document translation that keeps formatting, and glossaries so brand names and domain terms are treated your way. When generic translations of your field's jargon aren't good enough, AutoML Translation trains a custom model on your parallel sentence pairs. The Media Translation API translates streaming speech directly, folding speech recognition and translation into one low-latency call. The book's use case: multilingual chat support, where customer and agent each read in their own language.

Natural Language AI: what is this text about, and how does it feel?

  
The Natural Language API has five main methods: sentiment (overall and per sentence, with a score from −1 to +1 and a magnitude for how much emotion), entities (people, places, products, with links to knowledge graph entries), entity sentiment (how the writer feels about each entity), syntax (tokens, parts of speech, dependency trees) and content classification into a category taxonomy. AutoML Natural Language trains custom classifiers and extractors.

Speech-to-Text: from audio to words

  
Speech use cases are either human-to-human (transcribe calls, meetings, video captions) or human-to-computer (voice commands, IVR). Speech-to-Text supports 125+ languages and variants, automatic punctuation, speaker diarization (who said what), word-level timestamps, a profanity filter, model adaptation with phrase hints for your vocabulary, and models tuned for phone calls or video. Contact Center AI: let people do the hard calls

  
Call volumes keep rising and customers hate waiting. Contact Center AI puts conversational AI into the contact center. Its core: Dialogflow virtual agents that understand intent and hold natural conversations 24/7; Agent Assist, which listens to live calls and suggests answers and knowledge articles to human agents; and CCAI Insights, which mines conversations for topics, sentiment and trends. 8
Document AI: turn paper into data

  
Business runs on documents: invoices, receipts, IDs, loan forms, contracts. Document AI combines computer vision (including OCR) and language understanding to classify documents, split bundles, and extract fields and tables into structured data. General processors read any document (OCR, form key-value pairs); specialized processors know particular forms (invoices, receipts, IDs, W-2s); industry solutions such as Lending DocAI and Procurement DocAI cover whole workflows. Call it synchronously for one document or in batch for thousands; send low-confidence fields to human review.

  

    

      

      

    

    

  






  
9
Recommendations AI: personal, at scale

  
Recommendations AI (part of Retail AI) uses Google's experience in deep-learning recommendation to personalize for each shopper, even with sparse data or a long tail of products, and adjusts in real time to what the shopper is doing. You feed it a product catalog and user events (views, add-to-cart, purchases) through the Retail API, pick a model type and an objective (clicks, conversions, revenue), and serve predictions on your pages. Recap on one page

  

    
SightVision API (labels, objects, OCR, faces, logos, SafeSearch) and Video Intelligence (time-coded annotations).

    
LanguageTranslation (glossaries, documents, AutoML, Media Translation) and Natural Language (sentiment, entities, syntax, classification).

    
SpeechSpeech-to-Text: sync up to ~1 minute, async for long files, streaming for live audio.

    
SolutionsContact Center AI, Document AI, Recommendations AI: several models packaged for one business job.

    
CustomEvery API has an AutoML path for your own labels and vocabulary.

    
RuleTry the pretrained API first, measure, and go custom only if it falls short.
