How the pages are drawnBlue marks Google Cloud services and what Google runs for you. Orange marks your choices and your share of the work. Green is a good outcome, red a risk. Anything that moves can be paused with your system's reduced-motion setting.
About the source. The book is mostly hand-drawn sketchnotes, so these pages work from its headings, captions and running text plus general knowledge of each service. The copy used was missing the pages of chapter 4 (Data Analytics); that page is rebuilt from the chapter's table of contents. The book dates from 2022; where a product has since been renamed or retired, the page says so.
