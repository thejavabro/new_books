1

Four books, four roles for Claude

The author argues that most people stall at "chatbot" use. The volume lays out a staircase instead: each book gives Claude a bigger role, and each ends with a hands-on project so you finish holding real work, not just theory. Tap a step.

Where to startNew to Claude? Read in order: you cannot automate a procedure until you can produce reliable work, and you cannot do that until you can hold a useful conversation. Already a regular user? Skim the chapter lists for Books 1 and 2 and jump ahead to where your skills stop.
2

The recurring boxes, and the Try it now habit

Every chapter uses the same small set of boxes, and ends with a Try it now task you can finish in five to fifteen minutes, followed by questions to check what Claude produced. That last part is the point: reviewing becomes a habit, not an afterthought.

Sample files, not homeworkUnlike many guides, the book ships practice files (a Word brief to summarize, a spreadsheet to analyse and so on) in the author's GitHub repository, so you can start a task immediately. You can download one file at a time or the whole repository as a ZIP. Your own files work too.
3

How a printed book copes with a fast-changing product

Menus, plan prices and feature names move every few months. The book's answer is to teach decisions that outlast the interface, and to pair every instruction with a stable landmark (what a menu is for) plus the label currently on screen. If the label changes, the landmark still gets you there. Volatile details live on an online changes page, organised by chapter.

5

Key terms: match the word to the job

The glossary has a few pairs that are easy to mix up. Dictate types your speech into the box; Voice is a spoken back-and-forth. Web search answers one current question; Research plans a multi-source, cited report. Chat is for focused questions; Cowork is for longer assignments that produce finished files. Sort these.

6

What you need before you start

You need very little: a device you already own (Windows, macOS, Chromebook, Linux, iOS or Android, in a browser or an app), a free account for nearly all of Book 1, and a Pro plan or higher from Book 2 onwards. The book covers Claude's Chat and Cowork experience; it does not teach coding with Claude Code.
