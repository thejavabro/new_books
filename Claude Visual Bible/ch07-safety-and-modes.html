Work Safely and Choose the Right Mode

Before Claude touches real work, decide what it may see and who checks the result. Then match the task to the right mode: a quick chat or a supervised assignment.

Book 2 starts with ground rules

From here on, conversations involve work: client information, company documents, decisions affecting other people. Book 2 uses Cowork and needs a paid individual plan or an organisation workspace. First find your employer's written AI policy (ask your manager or IT). If none exists, use this chapter as a sensible default and check sensitive material with your manager. Where the policy and the book differ, the policy wins.

2

Sort information before you share it

The book suggests three groups. When unsure, treat it as red. Red stays out unless your employer has approved a workspace built to handle it.

GreenPublic or general: messaging goals, target audience, published facts.
AmberInternal or identifying: client names, internal project details.
RedPasswords, private keys, unpublished financials, health records, personal data, anything under NDA.

4

From suggestion to action: where you must approve

Claude now does more than answer. Cowork and connected tools can edit files, send messages and complete tasks for you. Facts, figures, quotations and calculations are drafts until checked (ask for sources or working). And every step that sends, publishes, spends, shares data or changes a system other people rely on needs your approval first. Slide along the range.

Disclosure and professionalsEmployers, clients, regulators, journals and schools may require you to say AI helped. Check before you submit. For medical, legal, financial, employment or safety questions, use Claude to prepare questions and understand terms, then take it to a qualified person.
5

Three modes in one app

Chat is for questions, discussion and short pieces you finish in one sitting while staying involved in every step. Cowork is for multi-step assignments that research several sources, work with a folder of files, and end in a finished document, sheet, deck or report; it plans, runs for minutes and notifies you. Code is for software (not covered by this book). The author separates Chat and Cowork mainly by how much you delegate.

6

Has your task outgrown Chat?

Tasks grow: one competitor question becomes five companies across ten criteria from three files. Tick the signs you recognise. When the task keeps growing, stop and move it: in a project, switch to Cowork in the same project so context carries over.

7

Folders: give the smallest useful sandbox

Cowork on the desktop can work directly in a local folder: read several related files, find information without you knowing which file holds it, create new documents beside the sources, and update files. Unlike an upload, it can write back. So choose the smallest folder that does the job, check what is inside, keep backups, and say which files to use and which to ignore.

9

Where the work runs, and how it calls you back

Dispatch (beta at writing) lets you assign Cowork tasks remotely, but they run on your desktop, so the computer must be awake and the app open. A cloud-hosted session runs on Anthropic's servers and keeps going when your computer is off. Turn on completion notifications in the desktop app's settings, but remember banners can show document titles to anyone glancing at your screen.
