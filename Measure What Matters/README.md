# Measure What Matters — visual companion

Interactive, illustrated learning pages for *Measure What Matters: OKRs, the Simple Idea that Drives 10x Growth* by John Doerr (Portfolio/Penguin, 2018). One self-contained HTML page per chapter group plus a Resources toolkit page.

## How to open
Open `index.html` in any browser. Every page works offline (fonts fall back to system fonts) and links to the others by relative path, so keep the files together in one folder. Light and dark themes (follows your system; the "Theme" button toggles), phone friendly.

## Pages
- `ch01-google-meet-okrs.html` — Ch 1, Google, Meet OKRs
- `ch02-father-of-okrs.html` — Ch 2, The Father of OKRs
- `ch03-operation-crush.html` — Ch 3, Operation Crush: An Intel Story
- `ch04-focus-commit.html` — Ch 4–6, Superpower #1: Focus and Commit (Remind, Nuna)
- `ch05-align-connect.html` — Ch 7–9, Superpower #2: Align and Connect (MyFitnessPal, Intuit)
- `ch06-track.html` — Ch 10–11, Superpower #3: Track for Accountability (Gates Foundation)
- `ch07-stretch.html` — Ch 12–14, Superpower #4: Stretch for Amazing (Chrome, YouTube)
- `ch08-cfrs.html` — Ch 15–16, Continuous Performance Management: CFRs (Adobe)
- `ch09-zume-pizza.html` — Ch 17, Baking Better Every Day: The Zume Pizza Story
- `ch10-culture.html` — Ch 18–20, Culture (Lumeris, Bono's ONE Campaign)
- `ch11-goals-to-come.html` — Ch 21, The Goals to Come, plus the Bill Campbell dedication
- `appendix-resources.html` — Resources 1–5: Google's OKR playbook, a typical cycle, conversation prompts, in sum, further reading

Each page has diagrams, step-through walkthroughs, small simulations and a quick-check quiz.

## Notes
- Explanations are original summaries written for this companion, not text from the book. Short quoted phrases only. Example OKRs marked "illustrative" or "invented" are not from the book; teaching models (e.g. the goal-difficulty curve) are sketches, not book data.
- Missing content: none. All 21 chapters and Resources 1–5 in the PDF were present and readable. The acknowledgments, endnotes and index were intentionally not reproduced (reference material).
- The Resources self-check saves ticks in your browser's local storage only.
