Spelling issues

Reconstructed

Misspellings happen at speed, and the Tools chapter says that's okay in the moment. But a misspelled speaker or company name in a shared image is not. Before sharing, check names against the schedule or the event site (your phone is in your kit for this), then cover errors with correction fluid or a white gel pen, or rewrite inside a container. Find the five errors.

3

What makes visual notes good?

"Good" is about content, not aesthetics. Information is worthless if you can't remember it or act on it, and even very simple visual notes can make it stick. Notes are like a puzzle: you only see the picture if all the pieces are there. They must be an accurate summary of the main points first; no lettering, shading or color can rescue an inaccurate summary. Your notes need to BE good before they can LOOK good. Remove pieces and see.
