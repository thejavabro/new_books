# The Art of Visual Notetaking: visual companion

Interactive study pages for *The Art of Visual Notetaking* by Emily Mills (Walter Foster Publishing, 2019). There is one page per chapter, or per small group of chapters. Each page has simple redrawn illustrations, interactive pieces (drawing pads, builders, sliders, sorting games, step-throughs) and a quick-check quiz.

## How to open
Unzip the folder and open `index.html` in any modern browser. Every page is self-contained and works offline. Fonts come from Google Fonts when you are online; without a connection the pages fall back to system fonts. Light and dark themes follow your system setting, and each page has a toggle button.

## About the content
- All explanations are original summaries written for this companion. Only short phrases are quoted. The drawings are simple original redraws, not copies of the book's art.
- The book is mostly illustration, and many pages have little or no text. The summaries are based on the book's headings, captions and body text, plus general sketchnoting knowledge where pictures carried the meaning. Where that matters, the page says so. For example, the closing "A Walkthrough" is image-only and is only described.
- Simulations and curves (memory, skill growth, smudge risk and so on) illustrate the book's arguments. They are not data from the book.
