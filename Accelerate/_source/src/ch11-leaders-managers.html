Leadership isn't about who reports to you; it's about inspiring and motivating the people around you. The team used a model with five characteristics of a transformational leader. Rate a leader you know on each (thinking of the survey's statements shown under each slider), and compare with the profile high-performing teams reported.

High-performing teams reported the strongest leadership on all five dimensions, and low performers the weakest. Teams in the bottom third for leadership were only half as likely to be high performers. But the top 10% of leaders were no more likely (even slightly less) to lead high performers than average. Leaders can't do it alone. Think of an amplifier.

Managers hold people, budgets and resources, so they can do even more. They connect strategy to the team's work, make the environment safe, invest in people, remove obstacles, make deployments less painful, show performance metrics aligned with goals, and delegate authority: give power to those with the knowledge. Investment in DevOps was highly correlated with delivery performance. What can investment look like?

Three things correlated strongly with delivery performance and built strong team culture: cross-functional collaboration, a climate for learning, and effective use of tools. Sort each practice into its lever.
