7

Finding high, medium and low performers

The performance groups weren't set by hand-picked cut-offs. Ward's hierarchical clustering grouped respondents by their delivery metrics, then ANOVA with Tukey's post-hoc tests confirmed the groups differ significantly (p < 0.10) on every metric. Chapter 2 animates the clustering; Chapter 12 explains the dendrogram.
