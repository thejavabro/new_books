    4

    Conclusion: you can't buy it, and you can't copy it

    The book closes on a simple message. High performance isn't something you can purchase as a tool, or copy from another company's org chart. It comes from building these capabilities, deliberately, in your own context, with a culture that lets people learn.

    The data also says it's possible for anyone: high performers exist in every industry, company size and kind of system, regulated or not, old or new. And the gap is widening, so the sooner you start, the better.
