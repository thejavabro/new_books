Three kinds of measures

Vague characteristics need objective definitions. The book splits measures into three groups. Click each.

Performance, scalability, elasticity: measured on the running system. Use statistics, not single numbers. Example: average page response under 500 ms, and alert when any request exceeds two standard deviations from the mean. Track first-contentful-paint and similar front-end flavours separately.

Code quality you can count: cyclomatic complexity, coupling, cohesion. Tools can compute them in the build.

Agility, testability and deployability show up in process: test coverage, deployment frequency, percentage of successful deployments, lead time for changes.

2

Cyclomatic complexity, counted live

Cyclomatic complexity (CC) counts independent paths through a function: CC = E − N + 2 for its graph, or more simply decisions + 1. Add decisions to the function and watch the score and the control-flow graph grow. The book suggests under 10 is generally acceptable, and under 5 is better.

Governance with fitness functions
