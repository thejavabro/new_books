The four Cs of architecture

Accidental complexity is complexity we add ourselves; essential complexity comes with the problem. Architects should avoid the first. The book's four Cs help. Click each.

Communication

Clear, effective communication of ideas and decisions.

Collaboration

Work with teams and stakeholders rather than above them.

Clarity

Be clear and concise; avoid ambiguity.

Conciseness

Say what matters; respect people's time.

4

Leading by example

Pragmatic, yet visionaryBalance future-looking ideas with budget, skills and time constraints. Don't over-engineer.
Use names and ask, don't tell"What if we tried…?" turns orders into collaboration. Remember people's names; firm handshakes and eye contact build trust.
Respect developer flowBatch meetings in the morning or end of day; protect deep-work time. Be available but not interrupting.

Meeting load

Add meetings to the architect's day and see what's left for the team. The book suggests attending only meetings where you add value, and asking for an agenda.
