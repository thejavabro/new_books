Microservices Architecture

Highly decoupled services, each a bounded context owning its own data. The most scalable and evolvable style, and the most complex and expensive.

Topology: bounded contexts, each with its own data

Microservices takes domain partitioning to the extreme. Each service models one bounded context (from domain-driven design) and owns its data; nobody else touches its database. Duplication is preferred over coupling. Toggle the operational layer.

Every service: its own code, its own data, its own deployment. Services talk via the API layer or each other's contracts.

Operational reuse: logging, monitoring and circuit breakers live in a sidecar next to each service, maintained by an infrastructure team. Connected sidecars form a service mesh, a consistent control plane across all services.

Choreography and orchestration

Communication can be synchronous (protocol-aware, heterogeneous, interoperable) or asynchronous. For workflows, choose between letting services coordinate themselves or using an orchestrator. Step through an error case.

4

Transactions across services

Don't do transactions in microservices: fix granularity instead. The book's advice: if you need ACID across services, your boundaries are probably wrong. When you truly need it, a saga coordinates compensating actions, but sagas are complex and fragile (recall fallacy #10).

Each step commits locally. No global lock.

A failure triggers compensating updates in reverse. Meanwhile other requests may have seen the intermediate state: the data was only eventually consistent.

Characteristics ratings

Microservices: characteristics scorecard

Stars follow our reading of the book's scorecard: 1 means poorly supported, 5 means a core strength. Check the book's figure for the authors' exact ratings.

Top marks for scalability, elasticity, evolvability and modularity, plus strong deployability and testability thanks to small, isolated services. Low on cost and simplicity; performance suffers from network hops and security checks between services.
