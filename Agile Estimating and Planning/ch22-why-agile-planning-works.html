Why Agile Planning Works

Why does all of this work? Planning is a search for the best mix of features, resources and schedule, and agile planning keeps searching. Here are the eight reasons it succeeds, and a dozen guidelines to take with you.

Why small, similar stories flow

Queuing theory says cycle time (start of work to value delivered) grows with variability. Same total work, same team, stories arriving at a steady pace. Only the mix of sizes differs.
