An Agile Approach

Agile teams work as one, in short timeboxed iterations, building the most valuable things first. They plan at several horizons and keep revising as new knowledge arrives.

1

One team, several roles

No throwing work over walls from analysts to coders to testers. Everyone shares the goal, but some roles matter especially for planning. Toggle the two pictures, then click a role.

2

Short iterations that deliver something

There's no big requirements phase, then design, then coding. Every timeboxed iteration (usually one to four weeks) does a little of everything and ends with coded, tested, potentially shippable features. Timeboxed means the iteration ends on time, even if a feature has to be dropped.

3

A flow of capabilities and knowledge

A project isn't just steps to execute. It produces two things: new capabilities (in the product) and new knowledge. Knowledge comes in two kinds: about the product (what users really want) and about the project (team, technology, risks). Sort these discoveries.

4

A 10K race or a 60-minute race?

Traditional plans treat a project like a 10K: the finish line is fixed, run there as fast as you can. An agile project is more like a timed race: run as far as you can in 60 minutes. We know when we finish, and we keep steering toward the best what.

5

You can't plan past the horizon

Standing in a boat, you can only see a few miles. On a 20-mile trip you must look up and correct course several times. Plans are the same: their accuracy drops fast past what you can see. Raise or lower your eyes.

6

The planning onion

Agile teams plan at three horizons they can see: the release, the iteration and the day. Outer layers (product, portfolio, strategy) usually belong to others. Click a layer.

7

Conditions of satisfaction

Every plan starts from what the product owner needs to call it a success: scope, schedule, budget (quality isn't up for negotiation). If no plan meets all of them, one of them has to give, and planning loops around again. Try to find a plan that works. This team finishes about 5 points per person per month.

For one iteration, conditions of satisfaction are the stories to build plus high-level tests. For "As a user, I can cancel a reservation" they might be:

Cancel 24 h+ aheadFull refund.
Cancel inside 24 hRefund minus a ¤25 fee.
ConfirmationCancellation code shown on screen and emailed.
