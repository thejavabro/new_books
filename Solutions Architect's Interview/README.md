# Solutions Architect's Interview: a visual companion

This is a set of 14 interactive HTML pages: an index plus one page per chapter. It covers *Solutions Architect's Interview: Winning strategies and effective tactics for interview success* (Saurabh Shrivastava, Neelanjali Srivastav, Dhiraj Thakur and Sanjeet Sahay, BitMaple, 2024). Each page has original diagrams and interactive widgets. For the question-heavy chapters, a page also has a representative set of interview questions with short model answers. Every page ends with a 3–5 question quiz.

## How to open

1. Unzip the archive.
2. Open `index.html` in any modern browser.

The pages work offline. Each page has a contents list, previous and next links, a link back to the index, and light, dark and auto themes. They also work at phone width. Quiz answers and checklists are saved only in your browser.

## About the content

All explanations, diagrams, examples and model answers are original summaries written for this companion. They quote only short phrases from the book. Where the book gives tips instead of answers, the short answers here are my own, and the page says so. This companion is not a replacement for the book.

## Missing or skipped content

- No chapter content is missing. All 13 chapters are covered.
- These parts of the book are intentionally not covered: front matter (title, copyright, contents, preface), the part divider pages, and the closing promotional pages for other books.
- PDF page 559 is blank (a separator between chapters 9 and 10), so nothing was lost there.
- Chapter 2: in the book, the printed answer to the mobile photo-app question describes Kubernetes orchestration instead. The ch02 page points this out and gives a matching answer.
