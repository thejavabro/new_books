Pick an Automation Building Block, Then Schedule It

Automate only what repeats and has settled down. Choose the plainest building block that works, then start with scheduled tasks: one-off, recurring, or a quiet watch that only speaks when a condition is met.

1

Spot work that is worth automating

A task is a candidate when it repeats, follows a pattern and takes real time to redo. Listen for "I did this last month too" or "I always have to remind it about our format". Multi-step tasks (gather, draft, check against a rule) gain most from structure, because a plain chat loses track of stages. But don't automate a task you've done only once: you can't yet tell which parts are stable.

2

Choose the plainest building block that works

The book's core question at every stage: what is the least complicated mechanism that reliably handles this work? Answer three questions about your task.

Connections and subagentsAdd an app or MCP connection only when the task depends on outside data or actions; one you don't need adds risk without value. Use subagents (separate specialists working in parallel) only when work splits cleanly into independent pieces such as research, drafting and review. The book's optional online chapters 3.9 (MCP) and 3.10 (workspace agents) are not in the printed volume.
3

When there is no connection: Appshots and Computer Use

Sometimes no plugin, app or MCP server exists for the tool you use. Then ChatGPT can work with the screen itself, but treat that as a last resort: a structured connection is faster, more reliable and easier to review than reading pixels.

Appshots

A one-time snapshot

Shares an image of the front app window plus any text it exposes (visible or hidden) as an attachment. Bring only the window you mean to share to the front.

Computer Use

ChatGPT drives the mouse and keyboard

For GUI-only tasks. It asks permission per app; stay present for anything involving accounts, passwords or payments. One clear app or task at a time.

Likely changed since publicationAs printed: Appshots were macOS-desktop-only (press both Command keys or a custom hotkey); Computer Use was a plugin installed from the directory in ChatGPT Work, on macOS and Windows, needing Screen Recording and Accessibility permissions on macOS; on Windows it took over your foreground input while running. All of this was early and platform-specific.

A durable order for building automation

5

Scheduled tasks: the work plus the trigger

Every scheduled task has two parts: what to do and when to do it. The trigger can be a time (once), a schedule (repeat), or a condition (check periodically and speak only when it's met). Try the prompt once in a normal chat first: scheduling an unclear prompt just repeats an unclear result. Build one.

Watch outMake sure a task was actually created: a confident "I'll do that" in ordinary text is not a schedule. Look for the task confirmation and check its date, time and description, then find it under Scheduled in the sidebar (where you can Run now, change notifications, Pause or Delete).
Likely changed since publicationThe book lists active-task limits by plan: Go 3, Plus 5, Business and Edu 10, Pro and Enterprise 15. At the limit you cannot create or resume a task until one is paused, deleted or completes. Limits and check frequencies are on OpenAI's tasks help page and change.
6

A watch should stay quiet until something happens

A recurring report returns a report every time. A monitoring task should be silent until its condition is met. The book's example is waiting for tickets. Run fourteen days of daily checks with and without the "don't notify me when nothing changed" rule, and with a stop date.

Not for emergenciesA watch checks on a schedule; it does not watch every second. Never rely on one for medical warnings, financial trades, legal deadlines or safety alarms. Use the official alert service.
7

Edit, pause or delete?

Tasks drift and keep running while nobody reads the results. Review them monthly (you could schedule a reminder to do it). When editing, change one thing and say what stays the same ("run every Friday at 8 AM instead of Thursday; keep location, sources, format and word limit"), then confirm the next run. ChatGPT may also pause unattended tasks, and deleting a linked chat may pause a task.
