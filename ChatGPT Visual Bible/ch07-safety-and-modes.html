Work Safely, and Pick Chat, Work or Codex

Work tasks raise the stakes. Follow your employer's policy, sort data into green, amber and red, keep a human approval before irreversible actions, and choose the mode (Chat, Work or Codex) that fits the size of the job.

1

Ground rules before the first work task

From Book 2 on, conversations involve clients, company documents and decisions that affect other people. You need no special settings, just two things: your employer's AI policy and a checking habit. If the policy conflicts with anything in the book, the policy wins. Policies change too: reread when your employer switches vendors or announces new rules.

Personal account

Belongs to you. Fine for your own life; some employers allow it for low-risk work such as brainstorming or learning.

Approved workspace

Belongs to your employer, run by an administrator with its own storage, retention and access rules. Required by many organisations for anything with customer or company data.

Check the label before you pasteThe two can look nearly identical. Look at the account or workspace name in the corner every time you start a work conversation.
Likely changed since publicationThe book says every chapter in Book 2 requires an individual paid plan or an organisation workspace. Which features need which plan is the most volatile detail in the volume.
2

Sort information into green, amber and red

Before sharing anything, sort it. Red (passwords, private keys, unpublished financials, health records, personal data about customers or colleagues, anything under NDA) stays out unless your employer has approved a workspace built for it. When unsure, treat it as red. The book's example: a marketing manager drafting captions from a client's campaign brief.

Green: share
Amber: remove or check
Red: keep out

Watch out: hidden passengersA pasted email thread, spreadsheet or screenshot can carry names, phone numbers and comments you never meant to share. Check the whole thing, not just the part you need.
3

Verify, disclose, and keep the approval step

Treat every factual claim as a draft: check numbers against the system of record, quotations against the original, totals by recalculating. Find out whether your employer, client, regulator or journal requires you to disclose AI help. And because Work and connected tools can now send, edit and complete tasks, put an approval point before anything that can't easily be undone. Climb the ladder of autonomy.

Medical, legal, financial, employment and safety questions still need a licensed person. Use ChatGPT to prepare: list questions, organise your situation, decode terms.

4

Three modes for three sizes of job

Chat is for questions, discussion, short writing, a quick analysis, and single-pass deep research: work you finish in one sitting and stay close to. Work is for larger assignments: research across many sources and files, connected accounts, and a finished document, spreadsheet, deck or report, often running for minutes while you do something else. Codex is for writing and changing software, and is outside this book. Sort the tasks.

5

Pick a model and effort for a Work assignment

Work offered a finer control than Chat: choose which model does the job and how hard it thinks. The book's advice: start most assignments on the balanced model, and raise one step only when it proves harder than expected or supports a decision worth extra care. Running everything at maximum burns allowance for no benefit. Tap a cell.

Likely changed since publicationNames here follow the book: Sol (hardest work; paid plans), Terra (everyday; all plans, and the only Work model on Free and Go), Luna (simple high-volume; paid plans), efforts from Light to Max, and a Fast 1.5x speed toggle. Model families are renamed and retired frequently; treat this grid as the shape of the decision, not the current menu.

Supervise a long assignment in seven steps

Good practiceKeep a short record of what you approved during a long assignment. If the result is wrong later, it tells you which step introduced the problem.
7

Notice when Work needs you

The most common reason a supervised assignment stalls is missing the one moment it pauses for approval. Turn on notifications before you start (Settings → General → Notifications, with separate switches for permission requests and questions). The desktop app also offered an Activity view (bell icon) and an optional floating "pet" whose look reflects the assignment that needs you most. Change the running chats and see what the pet shows.

Watch outBanners and pets can reveal a document title or a finished step to anyone glancing at your screen. On confidential work or shared screens, limit alerts to permission and question notifications.
Likely changed since publicationPets, Activity view and the notification settings were new desktop features in mid-2026. Expect them to have moved, been renamed, or gone.
