Search, Deep Research and Your Own Files

Training data has a cutoff. Web search fetches current facts, deep research runs a planned multi-source investigation, and uploads let ChatGPT work on your documents. Each one hands you citations or extractions to verify.

When to searchCitationsDeep researchYour filesQuick check
1

When does a question need the web?

ChatGPT's built-in knowledge stops at a training cutoff. Anything that changes (prices, opening hours, news, availability, who holds a job) needs a live web search. It often searches automatically when you write words like "today", "latest", "current" or "price"; you can also ask explicitly, or switch on Web search from the + menu. Decide for each question.

Search, or memory is fine?

Good practiceIf an answer could be out of date, ask it to search rather than assuming it did. Ask for at least three sources, including one official or primary source. If every source repeats the same original article, ask for a wider search.
2

A citation is where it looked, not proof

Search answers carry small numbered citations; hovering shows the page, and a Sources panel (under the … menu below the answer) lists everything used. But a citation only shows where ChatGPT looked. It can summarise a page wrongly or attach the wrong page to a claim. Open the source and check three things: the date, whether it supports this specific claim, and whether quoted words match exactly.

3

Deep research: a planned investigation

Deep research doesn't answer straight away. It plans sub-questions, reads many sources over several minutes and returns a structured, cited report. Use it when one search can't do the job: comparing many options, a topic with conflicting evidence, a briefing that needs several sources. You stay in control at two points: the scope at the start and the review at the end.

Scope it: add one ingredient at a time

Watch outA longer, more thorough-looking report is not automatically more accurate. Deep research can misread a source or overstate a weak finding. Treat it as a strong first draft: open a few citations, check they say what the report claims, and note their dates.
Likely changed since publicationThe book says deep research is switched on from the + menu in Chat mode only (not Work, where multi-step research is the default behaviour), that the Free plan gets a small number of lightweight runs a month, and that the plan step offers Edit and Start buttons. Allowances, the lightweight/full split and the mode split are all likely to have moved.
4

Add documents, images and data

Use + → Add photos & files to attach PDFs, Word files, images, Excel or CSV files, or pick from connected cloud storage. Uploading isn't enough: explain the assignment as you would to a colleague, with the purpose, the reader and the format. "Here's a contract" gets a generic summary; "Summarise the tenant's obligations in plain language as a checklist" gets something usable.

Spot the extraction error

You asked for every date, amount and named party from a short agreement. Compare the extraction with the source and click the cell that is wrong.

Keep the original open beside the result: that's how you catch misread numbers, dropped rows and merged cells.

Watch outNo passwords, private keys, medical records or other people's personal information unless you have the right to share them. Handwriting, glare and blur reduce accuracy, so check transcribed numbers and names against the image.
