What ChatGPT Can Do, and Your Account

ChatGPT started as a chat box and grew into a work platform. Before you rely on it, know which tasks suit it, which stay with you, and how to set up an account whose plan and privacy settings you actually chose.

1

From chatbot to work platform

The back-and-forth chat is still the centre (hence the name), but around it ChatGPT can now speak and listen, look at photos, read uploaded files, search the web, connect to other services, run deep research, hold projects, produce finished deliverables in Work mode and run scheduled tasks. The book draws this as a path a single conversation can grow along. Press play, or tap any stage.

Dashed border = a stage where you, not ChatGPT, must decide.

The division of labour stays the same at every stage: you choose the goal, supply context, set limits and review; ChatGPT supplies speed and broad general knowledge. Responsibility for purpose and final decision never moves.

2

Tasks ChatGPT handles well, and what you check

A good task is one where you can describe the result and check the response: enough information, a clear audience, a useful format, and consequences you can live with if the answer is incomplete. The book sorts suitable tasks into six kinds. Each one comes with its own check.

Good practicePair AI help with a check you know how to perform. If you can't judge the output yourself, ask someone qualified to review it.
3

Where a person must stay in control

ChatGPT can produce a polished answer that contains an error: it may misunderstand your goal, skip an option, misread a file, or sound more certain than it should. Keep a person firmly in control whenever a task touches one of six conditions. Sort these examples.

Hand it over, or keep a human in charge?

Before using any response, run it through the book's three questions. Toggle the answers for a draft you have in mind.

Watch outA neat table, a citation or a professional tone can make a weak answer look dependable. Format is not correctness: open the sources.
4

From research to professional value

A long response is not value. Value is a person understanding a problem, making a sound choice, or finishing a task with less waste, and you need evidence of it. The book's running example is a community arts centre that wants more people at weekend workshops. Slide through how its use of ChatGPT could grow, and what it would measure.

The same rule applies commercially: a client pays for a dependable result, not for the number of prompts. Trust comes from understanding the problem, protecting information, checking the work and owning the deliverable. Start with a small, low-risk task you already know, and compare time and quality with your usual way.

5

Create and protect an account

You can try ChatGPT signed out, but an account keeps chats and settings together across devices. Use a personal account for personal life and your employer's managed account for approved work, and keep the two apart. Step through the setup.

Interface language is separateSettings → General → Language changes ChatGPT's menus and buttons (or Auto-detect from your device). It does not change the language ChatGPT replies in; that follows the language you write in unless you ask otherwise.
6

Free or paid? Upgrade on evidence, not impulse

The book's rule: start free, and upgrade only when you can name the repeated need a paid plan would solve, ideally after the same useful task has been interrupted by a limit more than once. Answer three questions.

Likely changed since publicationThe book lists individual plans as Free, Go, Plus and Pro with Business and Enterprise for organisations, says Plus costs the author about US$20 a month, and notes that some Free users in some regions see ads. Plan names, prices, ads, which tools each plan gets, and whether tools such as image creation or deep research share one allowance are exactly the details the author moved to his online changes page. Check chatgpt.com/pricing for your country.
7

Privacy is several switches, not one

Chat history, model improvement, memory, account administration and retention are separate controls. Changing one does not change the others. Flip the controls and watch what actually happens to a new conversation.

Watch outA managed work account is not private from the organisation that runs it: an administrator may be able to access, export, audit, retain or delete its data. And no setting makes it acceptable to paste passwords, private keys, health records, confidential files or other people's personal data you have no right to share.
Likely changed since publicationRetention details (the book quotes OpenAI saying temporary chats are deleted after 30 days, possibly reviewed for abuse), the exact name of the "Improve the model for everyone" switch, and the Settings path (Settings → Data Controls; Settings → Security and login for multi-factor authentication and passkeys) all live on OpenAI's help pages and change without notice.
