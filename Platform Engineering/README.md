# Platform Engineering, Drawn Out

A visual, interactive companion to *Platform Engineering: A Guide for Technical, Product, and People Leaders* by Camille Fournier and Ian Nowland (O'Reilly, 2024). It has one page per chapter, with diagrams, simulators and a short quiz on each.

## How to open

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. It works offline: the pages are self-contained and link to each other by relative paths. Only the Google Fonts stylesheet is loaded from outside, and the pages fall back to system fonts without it. A light/dark toggle sits in each page header.
