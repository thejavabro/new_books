Classic AI, generative AI, agentic AI

The chapter treats these as connected layers, not rival technologies. Classic AI classifies and predicts. Generative AI, built on large language models (LLMs), creates text, code and images on request. Agentic AI adds goals: it plans, calls tools and acts across systems, asking a human when it must. Compare them on one capability at a time.

Lending exampleA generative assistant can explain a loan product. An agentic one can collect the applicant's details, retrieve the policy, call underwriting services, prepare a recommendation and escalate to a human for approval.
2

The transformer and self-attention

RNNs and LSTMs read text one token at a time, which is slow and forgets long-range context. The 2017 paper "Attention Is All You Need" dropped recurrence: every token looks at every other token at once, in parallel. Click a word to see (illustrative) attention weights: how much it "looks at" the others to work out its meaning.

Encoder and decoder

The encoder builds a rich representation of the input. The decoder generates output token by token, attending to its own previous tokens and, through cross-attention, to the encoder's representation. Parallel training made transformers fast and scalable enough to become the base of today's generative AI.

Agents work as a team under an orchestrator

Serious agentic systems are rarely one super-agent. They are specialists coordinated by an orchestrator. In the chapter's warehouse example the goal is to fix a stock imbalance. Press play.

Principles behind the architectureAutonomy within explicit boundaries; adaptability mostly through architecture (memory, retrieval, feedback) rather than retraining; human-in-the-loop at decisive points; interoperability with enterprise tools, for example through MCP; and security and compliance built in from the start.

7

Agent protocols: a shared language

Agents built on different frameworks cannot talk without shared rules for messages, roles and sequencing. Protocols provide the communication layer; they do not orchestrate. Click a protocol to see where it sits.

The protocols were still young when the book was written; expect them to evolve. Benefits claimed: interoperability, less custom integration, standardization on familiar web technology such as JSON and HTTPS.

8

Agentic frameworks

A framework gives scaffolding: agent roles and boundaries, task and workflow management, memory, tool integration and monitoring. Pick what you are building to see which one the chapter's descriptions point to.

9

Retrieval-augmented generation, plain and agentic

RAG lets a model answer from fresh, authoritative sources instead of only from what it memorized in training. Agentic RAG splits retrieval and response across specialist agents under an orchestrator that enforces policy.
