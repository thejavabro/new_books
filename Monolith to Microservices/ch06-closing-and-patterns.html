Closing Words and Pattern Index

The book ends with two messages: decide rationally for your own context, and change incrementally. Then it lists every pattern. Here they are as a picker that suggests patterns for your situation.

1

Two messages to take with you

Message 1Decide rationally, in your contextGive yourself space and the right information. Don't copy others; think about your problem, weigh the options, move forward, and stay open to changing course.
Message 2Adopt incrementallyOf microservices, and of the technologies and practices around them. No two architectures are alike; manageable steps let you adapt as you go.

Microservices are definitely not for everyone. The hope is that you now know better whether they are right for you, and if they are, how to begin.
