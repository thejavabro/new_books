1

Why software delivery keeps hurting

The book's diagnosis: most organizations have an unhelpful model of what software development is. An obsession with "feature delivery" ignores the human and team dynamics; Conway's law is ignored or fought; re-orgs destroy capability; and a bewildering range of frameworks leaves team behaviour unspecified, producing either too-tight coupling or an isolationist autonomy that doesn't scale. Tap a problem to see the Team Topologies answer.

The model in one picture

Team-first thinking at the centre; four team types and three interaction modes around it; Conway's law applied on purpose; and evolution driven by sensing. Interactions outside the three modes are waste, a sign of poorly chosen boundaries or unclear purpose. Tap a ring.

The garden needs soil and water

Team Topologies is like instructions for placing plants and patterns for pruning. But a garden also needs soil, water and fertilizer, and a purpose. Without the four ingredients below, even well-placed teams won't thrive. Adjust them.
