Digital Transformation

Digital transformation changes the business model, not just the systems. Enterprise Architecture gives it a map, a sequence and a way to stay on course.

1

Digital transformation is a business shift

Digital transformation means weaving digital technology into every part of an organization to raise efficiency, improve experiences and drive innovation. It is more than buying software. It changes how the organization operates and delivers value, and it never really finishes. Five dimensions recur.

2

Digitization, digitalization, transformation

These three words are often used as if they meant the same thing, and that causes confusion. Step up the ladder with one example: a hospital's patient records.

3

Which comes first: EA or transformation?

The two disciplines need each other. Transformation projects reveal what is possible and reshape the target. EA supplies the blueprint and operating model that keep scattered initiatives coherent. A strategy built without the business underneath is a house without foundations. Compare the options.

4

A structured approach through the ADM

TOGAF gives transformation its phases. Tap a phase to see its job in a digital transformation.

5

Case: an 18-month hospital transformation

Government policy pushed the hospital toward telehealth, remote monitoring and self-medication: the right care in the right place. The transformation ran in five phases. Step through it and watch the ADM phases and the KPIs move. The KPI curves are illustrative. The book reports only the end result.

    30%lower healthcare costs
    24/7patient information services

    The savings came mostly from moving non-acute patients to home care. Telehealth, wearables and AI assistants let doctors spend more time on acute cases. Staff came to see technology as an ally rather than an obstacle.
