Meet Academic Diagnostic Medicine

The book's capstone is a fictional, fairly architecture-mature hospital moving toward value-based care. Its in-house Enterprise Architect, Jim, has to turn the board's strategy into capabilities and see the change through. The story runs from March 2027 to August 2030 and covers every ADM phase. Some phases repeat, and sometimes Jim steps briefly into a later phase and comes back.

Plateaus, work packages and the plan

Jim plans the route from baseline to target through two Transition Architectures. Work package types are A (new capabilities), B (improve capabilities) and C (processes), and each is tied to a goal. The Implementation and Migration Plan turns them into three phases. Phase 2 depends on staff training for the new Planning Application.

Readiness check, January 2028

Employee engagement and skills scored only 3. Automating manual discharge planning was the likely cause. The answer was training for the Planner role.

6

January 2030: the board asks how it is going

Jim answers with a heat map on his Driver/Goal/Objective diagram. Two objectives are still behind. Tap them to see why.

Lesson: the ADM is not a straight line. Jim iterates, steps ahead and comes back, reuses repository content, and keeps stakeholders close at every stage. The architecture succeeds because of that steady attention, not because of the template.
