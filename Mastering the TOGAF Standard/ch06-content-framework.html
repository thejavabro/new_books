Content Framework

The Content Framework is a filing system for architecture work. It says what kinds of outputs exist, how they nest, and which ADM phase produces them.

1

Three things to settle in the Preliminary phase

When an organization sets up its own Architecture Capability, three things need defining, in order. First what kinds of things exist, then a way to file the work, then the specific artifacts. The Content Framework is the middle one: a categorization framework, a high-level summary sitting on top of the detailed metamodel.

2

Five jobs the Content Framework does

Each job is a promise to the people who read architecture: same deliverable, same shape, same meaning every time. Tap to open.

3

The Extended Content Framework, phase by phase

Look at the Content Framework from far away and you see the ADM. Each section lines up with one or more phases, and the Extended version lists the metamodel entities that belong in each. Tap a section.

An inconsistency worth knowingDriver, goal, objective and measure belong to the Business Architecture section of the metamodel. In the Extended Content Framework they sit in a separate Motivation box instead. The author suspects many hands on the standard; just be aware the two models disagree.
The exception in Implementation GovernanceIts entities (standards, guidelines, specifications) don't come from the metamodel at all. They are taken from the Architecture Repository.
4

Then and now

The current Content Framework is meant to be more modular, more adaptable and more focused on outcomes than earlier versions. Flip between the two mindsets.

5

When the content is allowed to change

The Content Framework is reference material, but the ADM's iterations refine it. Some phases are natural moments to add or change content. Tap a phase on the wheel.

6

A missing artifact: the Work Package Portfolio Map

Both models contain the work package entity, yet the standard hardly uses it beyond the Project Context Diagram. The author recommends adding a Work Package Portfolio Map: all initiatives and the goals and objectives they serve. It is the bridge between business goals and the Architecture Roadmap in phases E and F. Tap any item to trace its links.
