The whole framework on one page

Five core concepts: the Governance Framework, the Architecture Repository, the ADM, the Enterprise Metamodel and the Content Framework. They are not a random collection. They depend on one another, and 13 relationships tie them together with building blocks, domains, roles and artifacts. Tap a concept to see its links, or step through all 13.

The real standard is far richer than this. The map only shows how the main parts fit together.

Seven myths, busted

The author's closing chapter takes on the seven most common myths. They come from not reading the framework closely, from relying on what people learned for an exam years ago, or from repeating hearsay. For each statement, decide: myth or fact?
