Tailoring and Agile Architecture

Tailoring is not optional: every organization has to fit the standard to itself. And agile teams need architecture too, at the right level of detail and at the right moment.

1

TOGAF is meant to be customized

The standard says plainly that it requires interpretation. Its concepts are the essential scaffolding. Everything else is an example or a starter set you may replace. There are five areas you can tailor. Compare the generic advice with the book's healthcare case.

A common reason practitioners abandon TOGAF is that it explains what you can do, not how, and that it has few real-world examples. The healthcare case is the author's answer to the second complaint.
2

A healthcare-tailored ADM

Healthcare must adopt telemedicine, AI diagnostics and other new technology quickly. So the tailored ADM puts more weight on some phases than others. Tap a highlighted phase.

3

Agile architecture is not an oxymoron

Agile is broader than the software manifesto. It means moving and changing quickly and easily to deliver value. Four traits keep coming back. Tap each one.

Sequential or overlapping?

A regular approach has five components. In agile working, sprints run inside every component, and the components overlap. You only need enough of the problem defined to give context. Then again, as with a painting, you cannot start without some idea of the picture.

4

Strategic, Segment, Capability

TOGAF partitions architecture work into three levels of granularity. Each maps to ADM phases and to agile or SAFe concepts. Choose a level.

5

From one transition to many sprints

A project goes from baseline through a transition to target. Partitioning splits the transition into several Transition Architectures. Each is a piece small enough to build in a few sprints and is at least a Minimum Viable Architecture: the minimum that can be realized and adds business value. Use the sliders.

Minimum Viable ArchitectureThe architecture side. The smallest realizable piece of architecture that adds business value. It is the equivalent of a partition.
Minimum Viable ProductThe delivery side. An output that meets a minimum set of functional and non-functional requirements and can run live.
6

Running Phase E sprint by sprint

Not every activity has to happen in every sprint. In practice, Phase E becomes a set of recurring checks. Step through one sprint cycle.

Phases B to E have no fixed order. Iterate across them as needed. Phase G confirms that contracts delivered the agreed capabilities. Phase H confirms that the expected benefits were actually realized.
