The ADM: Preliminary and Vision

The Architecture Development Method is the heart of the standard: a cycle of phases from setting up the practice to managing change. This page covers the cycle as a whole and its first two phases.

1

The heart of the standard

Everything in TOGAF can be put into practice by running the Architecture Development Method. It is often mistaken for a process. It is better seen as a toolbox grouped into phases: every step is a possible step, steps may be skipped or adapted, and even their order is not fixed. Each phase has the same four parts: objectives, steps, outputs and an approach. Tap a phase for its objective.

The most common misunderstandingMany architects assume everything in the ADM must be done. The author calls this "completely wrong and misguided". Tailoring is paramount; skipping steps, for instance in agile work, is supported and even encouraged.

Four documents frame every cycle

Besides the artifacts that describe the architecture, a cycle produces four documents that describe the work: what is asked, what is agreed, what is designed and what is contracted. Step through one cycle.

The architecture team and who does what

An architecture team is not "people with architect in their title". It can include project managers and senior managers, as long as they grasp what architecture is about. Its make-up follows the positioning: inside IT, mostly architects and technical experts; under the board, an Enterprise Architect with senior managers and portfolio managers. Phase A: Architecture Vision

Phase A builds a high-level vision and makes sure stakeholders agree with it and with the business requirements. It runs as a real project inside the organization's project framework, with formal recognition, leadership endorsement and line-management support. Step through it.
