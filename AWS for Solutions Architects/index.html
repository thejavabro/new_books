How to use these pagesEach page explains a chapter in plain words, then lets you poke at it: click parts of a diagram, step through a flow, move a slider, or sort services into the right bucket. Every page ends with a short quiz and a recap. Service limits and prices quoted in the book date from 2023, so treat numbers as examples.
Note on the source fileThe PDF these pages were built from was saved under the wrong name, "Kafka The Definitive Guide.pdf". Its contents are AWS for Solutions Architects, Second Edition, and these pages cover that book. Every chapter was present in the file.
