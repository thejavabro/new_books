## Cross-Category Notes

- **The Phoenix Project** (DevOps #1) is a novel — read it early, even before Category 2, to get the "why" behind all the process work.
- **Designing Data-Intensive Applications** (Distributed #2) is the single most important technical book on the list. Don't skip it.
- **Accelerate** (DevOps #4) is short and data-driven — pairs well right after The DevOps Handbook.
- **Latency** (Distributed #5) reads best after DDIA and Database Internals, once you know where the time actually goes.
- **Mastering the TOGAF Standard** (Architecture #7) is enterprise-architecture process, not system design. Read it only if you're heading toward an EA or principal-architect role; it also sets up Transformation Enterprise Architecture with Agentic AI (AI #12).
- **Category 6 (AI)** assumes you've read the architecture and distributed systems categories first, since agentic systems are just distributed systems with LLMs inside them.
- **Category 7 (Leadership)** can be read in parallel with anything once you're managing a team — it's not a prerequisite for the technical books.
