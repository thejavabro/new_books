Part V introduction: the Third Way

Part III built flow. Part IV built feedback, as early, fast and cheaply as possible. Part V adds the Third Way: practices that create chances to learn as quickly, often, cheaply and early as possible. That includes learning from accidents and failures, which are inevitable in complex systems, and organizing work so that we are always experimenting and making the system safer.

The payoff the introduction names is higher resilience and a growing shared knowledge of how the system really works. Each turn of the loop below adds to it.

Part V conclusion and further reading

The conclusion recaps the themes: learning from incidents, creating shared repositories and sharing what we learn. All of these matter in complex systems, and they make the culture more just and the systems safer and more resilient. Part VI goes on to apply flow, feedback and learning to Information Security.
